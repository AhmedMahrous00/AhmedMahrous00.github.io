## Research

2026:

### [The reflexivity threshold: A phase transition for multi-agent performative prediction](https://amahrous.com/papers/reflexivity-threshold/)

Ahmed Mahrous and [Roberto Di Pietro](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/profiles/roberto-di-pietro)

*NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted* · ICORE A\*

Under the stated sensitivity and curvature assumptions, a reflexivity matrix with spectral radius below one guarantees convergence of repeated retraining. Above one, we construct noisy systems where every local-information learner incurs expected cumulative excess loss growing linearly for horizons up to order 1/(ρ(Γ) − 1).

### [FinMoji: A framework for emoji-driven sentiment analysis in financial social media](https://amahrous.com/papers/finmoji/)

Ahmed Mahrous, Jens Schneider, and [Roberto Di Pietro](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/profiles/roberto-di-pietro)

*ACM Transactions on the Web, 20(3), Article 35* · Q1 · IF 4.9

On about 528,000 emoji-containing StockTwits posts, emoji-only sentiment models reach F1 ≈ 0.75, versus ≈ 0.88 for text-and-emoji models, with substantially lower computational cost. Financial emoji usage also differs from general social-media usage.

[PDF](https://amahrous.com/papers/finmoji/paper.pdf) · [HTML](https://amahrous.com/papers/finmoji/#full-text) · [BibTeX](https://amahrous.com/papers/finmoji/citation.bib)

### [Cross-cultural transfer of emoji semantics and sentiment in financial social media](https://amahrous.com/papers/cross-cultural-emojis/)

Ahmed Mahrous and [Roberto Di Pietro](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/profiles/roberto-di-pietro)

*Findings of ACL 2026*

Across financial communities in four languages, emoji frequencies vary while meanings and sentiment polarity remain largely stable. Including emojis reduces sentiment-transfer gaps relative to text-only models, though cross-language transfer remains the hardest setting.

[PDF](https://amahrous.com/papers/cross-cultural-emojis/paper.pdf) · [HTML](https://amahrous.com/papers/cross-cultural-emojis/#full-text) · [BibTeX](https://amahrous.com/papers/cross-cultural-emojis/citation.bib)

### [What prediction-market contracts reveal at launch: Trading volume and dispute risk](https://amahrous.com/papers/prediction-market-launch/)

Ahmed Mahrous and [Roberto Di Pietro](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/profiles/roberto-di-pietro)

*ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2026)* · Oral · ~5% oral acceptance

Across 802,863 resolved Polymarket markets, launch-time contract information predicts trading activity, but demand prediction weakens sharply across periods of platform change. Dispute scores help rank groups of markets; the low dispute rate limits predictions for individual contracts.

[PDF](https://amahrous.com/papers/prediction-market-launch/paper.pdf) · [HTML](https://amahrous.com/papers/prediction-market-launch/#full-text) · [BibTeX](https://amahrous.com/papers/prediction-market-launch/citation.bib)

### [PumpSense: Real-time detection and target extraction of crypto pump-and-dumps on Telegram](https://amahrous.com/papers/pumpsense/)

Ahmed Mahrous and [Roberto Di Pietro](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/profiles/roberto-di-pietro)

*IEEE International Conference on Blockchain and Cryptocurrency (ICBC 2026)* · 18% acceptance

Using over 280,000 Telegram posts, we benchmark pump-announcement detection and extraction of the target coin and exchange. LightGBM reaches F1 = 0.79 and BGE-M3 reaches 0.83; LLMs perform best on target extraction.

[PDF](https://amahrous.com/papers/pumpsense/paper.pdf) · [HTML](https://amahrous.com/papers/pumpsense/#full-text) · [BibTeX](https://amahrous.com/papers/pumpsense/citation.bib)

### [TSTR for financial fraud: Learning to detect manipulation without real data](https://amahrous.com/papers/tstr-financial-fraud/)

Ahmed Mahrous and [Roberto Di Pietro](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/profiles/roberto-di-pietro)

*ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2025)* · Oral · 15.5% acceptance

A classifier trained only on synthetic pump-and-dump time series achieves 79.75% accuracy on real data. Adding 20% real training data raises accuracy to 88.1%, while simple Gaussian sampling outperforms the deep generative models tested.
