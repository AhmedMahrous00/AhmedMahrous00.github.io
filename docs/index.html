I work on artificial intelligence and decentralized finance. I study how learning algorithms, incentives, and strategic behavior shape markets, while also exploring how AI models can make reliable predictions.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at KAUST, advised by Roberto Di Pietro. My research combines mathematical modeling, empirical analysis, and machine learning, with work on AI methodology as well as applications to stablecoins, prediction markets, financial manipulation detection, and investor sentiment.
