2026:

The reflexivity threshold: A phase transition for multi-agent performative prediction

Ahmed Mahrous and Roberto Di Pietro

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted · ICORE A*

In multi-agent performative prediction, network feedback determines whether retraining converges. Under the stated sensitivity and curvature assumptions, a reflexivity matrix with spectral radius below one guarantees convergence; above one, we construct systems where every local-information learner suffers linearly growing cumulative excess loss.

2026:

FinMoji: A framework for emoji-driven sentiment analysis in financial social media

Ahmed Mahrous, Jens Schneider, and Roberto Di Pietro

ACM Transactions on the Web, 20(3), Article 35 · Q1 · IF 4.9

On about 528,000 emoji-containing StockTwits posts, emoji-only sentiment models reach F1 ≈ 0.75, versus ≈ 0.88 for text-and-emoji models, with substantially lower computational cost. Financial emoji usage also differs from general social-media usage.

2026:

Cross-cultural transfer of emoji semantics and sentiment in financial social media

Ahmed Mahrous and Roberto Di Pietro

Findings of ACL 2026

Across financial communities in four languages, emoji frequencies vary while meanings and sentiment polarity remain largely stable. Including emojis reduces sentiment-transfer gaps relative to text-only models, though cross-language transfer remains the hardest setting.

2026:

What prediction-market contracts reveal at launch: Trading volume and dispute risk

Ahmed Mahrous and Roberto Di Pietro

ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2026) · Oral

Across 802,863 resolved Polymarket markets, launch-time contract information predicts trading activity, but demand prediction weakens sharply across periods of platform change. Dispute scores help rank groups of markets; the low dispute rate limits predictions for individual contracts.

2026:

PumpSense: Real-time detection and target extraction of crypto pump-and-dumps on Telegram

Ahmed Mahrous and Roberto Di Pietro

IEEE International Conference on Blockchain and Cryptocurrency (ICBC 2026) · 18% acceptance

Using over 280,000 Telegram posts, we benchmark pump-announcement detection and extraction of the target coin and exchange. LightGBM reaches F1 = 0.79 and BGE-M3 reaches 0.83; LLMs perform best on target extraction.

2025:

TSTR for financial fraud: Learning to detect manipulation without real data

Ahmed Mahrous and Roberto Di Pietro

ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2025) · Oral · 15.5% acceptance

A classifier trained only on synthetic pump-and-dump time series achieves 79.75% accuracy on real data. Adding 20% real training data raises accuracy to 88.1%, while simple Gaussian sampling outperforms the deep generative models tested.
